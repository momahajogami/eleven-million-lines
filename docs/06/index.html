Languages and Theory

Before C there was assembly. Before assembly, punch cards. Before punch cards, a loom.

BASIC (access and democracy), Assembly with punch cards and the Jacquard loom, LISP (Church's lambda calculus made executable), ML/Haskell (type theory), Prolog (logic as computation). Primary sources: Lovelace 1843 (Note G), Church 1936, Turing 1936 and 1950, McCarthy 1960.
