Richard Stallman

The prophet. The printer. The four freedoms.

The GNU Manifesto, the GPL, Emacs, GDB, Bison. Stallman decided that the answer to "who owns the tools?" was: nobody. Everybody. You. The legal and moral infrastructure of free software runs through everything that follows.
