Alexander Grothendieck. William Lawvere. The mathematics that underlies the mathematics.

Unit 07 asks what it looks like when mathematics becomes structural — when the question shifts from "what is this object?" to "what are the relationships between objects?" Category theory, algebraic topology, and the proof assistants that make this mathematics machine-checkable. Grothendieck as the mystic who rewrote algebraic geometry from scratch. Lawvere as the logician who showed that logic and geometry are the same thing.

The unit is also a bridge: from pure abstraction to the applied topology of Unit 08, and from there to Carmack's BSP trees in Unit 09. Quake is applied Grothendieck, without knowing it.

## Materials

- **GROTHENDIECK.md** — the character study and the mathematical argument
- **quake.md** — bridge document: BSP trees as applied topology, Unit 07 to Unit 09
- **Lean/Mathlib** (`lean-mathlib/`) — the Lean proof assistant's mathematics library. Category theory, algebra, topology — all machine-verified.
- **Agda** (`agda/`) — a dependently-typed proof assistant. The type is the theorem; the program is the proof.
- **NumPy** (`numpy/`) — linear algebra at scale. The applied counterpart to the pure theory.
- **GSL** (`gsl/`) — GNU Scientific Library. Linear algebra, eigenvalues, FFT in C.
